Wall Street closed down 3.70 percent on the S&P 500 and 5.28 percent on the Nasdaq Composite, in the close account filed below, completing the worst week since March 2020 for two of the three indexes and a fall of 17.1 percent for the chip index, its worst week since 2001, in the weekly account filed below. Washington at Midnight: One Term for the Guardrails, and Two Tellings of the Huang Question

The noon conference’s one announcement gained its content on Friday from the Treasury Secretary, speaking after the return from Shenzhen. President Trump and President Xi agreed that humans should always be involved in military decisions that use artificial intelligence, and that no major military operation should be launched without a human being involved, Mr. Bessent told CNBC, in the Secretary’s account above, calling the agreement among the trip’s major achievements and saying it would stand apart from the two countries’ competition in the technology. That is the whole of the guardrails’ stated content at midnight: no text, no timetable, and no negotiating agency stands in the file, and no confirmation in Beijing’s own words stands in the file either; an earlier embassy statement carried in the week’s file, that the two sides should strengthen communication and keep the technology under human control, is the nearest parallel. Mr. Bessent added the chip position in the same interview: the H200 remains the most advanced chip China may purchase under licence, and Chinese orders for it have been few. The President’s own conference stands as the evening printed it—the trip a resounding success, data centres to be built at home and penalised abroad, only older chips for China—in the conference account filed below.

On the chipmaker’s former chief, Friday produced two tellings that do not meet, and the desk prints both. The President said he had discussed Mr. Huang with President Xi, that President Xi agreed Mr. Huang was a brilliant man, and that Mr. Huang had made some bad decisions: “I would like to see Jensen make a big apology and go back…we’re a country of second chances,” in the conference report filed below. Mr. Bessent said the allegations involving Mr. Huang did not come up in the bilateral meeting, in the Secretary’s account. The board’s stated position, against both tellings, is the one in the New York file: no reinstatement intended, a search under way. Settled: the guardrails’ one term, stated by the Secretary. Open: their text, Beijing’s confirmation, and which telling of the Huang conversation is the record.

Kyiv at Midnight: Nine Dead, Restricted Traffic, and an Answer Made of Words

The night’s count finished where the evening left it, and the midnight file records the finished figures and the day’s military reading together. At least nine people were killed: seven in Kyiv and two in other regions, in the latest account above, with the injured at 30 across Ukraine in the Interior Minister’s count and 17 in Kyiv in the mayor’s; a second full account puts the dead at at least eight, six in Kyiv and two in Lviv—the desk prints the spread in the Cocked Hat. The package stands at 27 ballistic missiles, 84 cruise missiles and 404 drones, the largest of November, in the assessment above, with air defences downing or suppressing three ballistic missiles, 79 cruise missiles and 380 drones and strikes recorded at 23 locations, in the latest account. The strike hit the Western Ukrainian 750-kilovolt substation, the largest in Europe, and forced the nuclear plants to reduce generation, with parts of Kyiv and ten regions without electricity, in the same account. Ukraine’s own strikes in the same hours hit three Russian ballistic missile launchers, destroying one, in the strike account filed below, and the Druzhba pumping station in Tatarstan on Thursday, damaging three storage tanks, in the latest account.

In Kyiv the repair advanced a step past the evening file. Traffic across the Podilskyi crossing was restored at about 3 p.m. local time, in the latest account, and the assessment records restricted traffic resumed across the damaged bridges generally; the Darnytskyi assessment continues and the Pivdennyi remains partly restricted, in the same account. In Poland the day produced the alliance’s fullest statement of what it did and did not do. Two Kh-101 missiles came down at Nowy Lipowiec and Rudnik nad Sanem and a jet-powered drone, possibly a Geran-5, at Molodycz, with no damage at any of the three, in the assessment; as many as 11 missiles and drones in Ukrainian airspace were tracked as potential threats to Poland’s, Polish, Swedish and Dutch aircraft were scrambled, and Poland and the alliance did not try to down the missiles and the drone because the conditions did not meet the rules of engagement, the operational commander said, in the same account. Prime Minister Tusk called the falls an intentional provocation; the Secretary General condemned the attack and the violation; France’s foreign minister called for a meeting of the Security Council; and Estonia will close its road border crossings with Russia on December 1, in the provocation account filed below and the latest account. President Zelensky’s reading stands against the week’s diplomacy, as the evening printed it: the attempt in Shenzhen to redraw the world had failed. UNICEF’s figures for the year stand beside the night’s: 1,144 children killed or injured between January and October. Settled: the count, the reopening in restricted form, and the non-interception, stated plainly by the commander who ordered it. Open: the Darnytskyi damage, the Council’s meeting, and the alliance’s answer beyond words.

The Gulf at Midnight: A Tanker Unnamed, a Weapon in Dispute, and Two Counts That Do Not Meet

Friday’s strike in the strait ends the day as it began it in the file: a ship with no name. A tanker on an outbound transit was struck on its port bow by an unknown projectile at about 1500 UTC, eight nautical miles north of Khasab, and its company security officer reported a fire on board, under Warning 214-26, in the fire account filed below; no casualties and no environmental impact were reported, and no later account in the file names the vessel, gives its flag or cargo, or reports the fire’s end. The Guards said the tanker caught fire after hitting a mine on an unauthorised transit; the mine explanation has not been independently verified, in the tanker account filed below. The week’s run now has a count of its own: seven strikes involving six vessels in the Gulf in the period, four of them tankers hit within 13 nautical miles of Khasab over three days, including the Tara Gas, attacked twice, in the surge account filed below, which puts maritime security incidents at their highest weekly level since mid-October.

The strait’s traffic ends the week with two counts that cannot both be the traffic. The desk prints the disagreement in the Cocked Hat without resolving it: the two instruments are not counting the same strait in the same way, and the file does not say how either counts. Oil’s week closed against its day: Brent settled Friday at $92.57 a barrel, up 0.64 percent, and December West Texas Intermediate at $83.98, up 0.93 percent, in the settlement account filed below, while the week’s readings put Brent down near 5 percent and the December contract down 6.5 percent, a third straight weekly decline, in the weekly account filed below, and United States crude futures down 19.2 percent for November, in the futures account. France and Spain at Midnight: A Law Passed, a Referral Promised, and a Verdict Confirmed

France’s file closed in the Senate and reopens at the Council. Senators gave final approval to the law presuming that police officers who use their weapons are acting lawfully, by 205 votes to 110, in the Paris account filed below; the law gives an officer who has shot someone the benefit of the doubt unless there is proof to the contrary. The Interior Minister, Mr. Nuñez, supports it as protection for officers in dangerous conditions; the United Nations special rapporteur on extrajudicial executions wrote ahead of the vote that the burden of disproving lawfulness should not fall on victims and their families; and the left-wing groups plan to refer the law to the Constitutional Council in a last effort to halt it, in the same account. No filing of that referral stands in the file at midnight: the referral is promised, not yet made. Settled: the vote and the text. Open: the referral, and the Council’s word.

Spain’s verdict, split on Thursday night, stands confirmed on two instruments at midnight, as the evening printed it: Mr. Feijóo the perceived winner of the debate, 51 percent to 29 percent in the Sigma Dos poll and 43 percent to 33 percent in the 40dB poll, before an audience of 10.2 million, in the Madrid account filed below. The standing instruments show an advantage for a bloc of the People’s Party and Vox; no post-debate voting-intention poll stands in the file. The second debate stands agreed for about November 26, and Spain votes November 29. Settled: the verdict. Open: whether it moves the vote.

The Air at Midnight: The Week Ends Without Its Root Cause

The week’s Washington promise stands unkept at the midnight stamp, as it stood at the evening’s. The Federal Aviation Administration said on Thursday that the government expected to announce this week the root cause of the November 13 ground stop; Friday’s file contains no announcement, and the week that contained the promise has now closed. The narrowed field stands as the evening printed it: a radar site at Amarillo, Texas, and a radio site at El Dorado, Arkansas, where the contractor L3Harris had been installing a new router in a copper-to-fibre upgrade; data flooding the network at 1,000 connection attempts every four to five seconds, destabilising 17 of the 22 en route centres, in the funding account filed below; the two routers at a laboratory in Florida for examination, and no evidence of a cyberattack, in the week account filed below. Secretary Duffy’s formulation remains the file’s firmest: the installation or the router was the bad actor. The installations stand paused and the new routers’ broadcasting function disabled. Congress is questioning the Secretary’s $50 billion modernisation request while the inquiry proceeds, in the funding account, and the Senate Commerce Committee is owed its technical answers by November 23—the first date of the Thanksgiving travel week, which now stands directly ahead of the unanswered question. Settled: the equipment, the two sites, and the precaution. Open: the root cause itself, a week and a day outstanding.
