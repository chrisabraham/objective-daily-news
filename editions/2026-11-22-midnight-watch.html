The Baltic at Midnight: A Fire Still Burning, a Tow Promised, and No Word That It Moved

The Baltic file ends Saturday one fact past the evening. The Sparta II, struck by two drones west of Gogland between 0840 and 0850 local time, anchored south of Loviisa at 1545, her 16 crew evacuated to a Russian ship and on their way home, was still burning late on Saturday with Finland leading the rescue operation, in the Baltic account filed below. The addition is the tow. Defence Minister Antti Hakkanen told Helsingin Sanomat that the Sparta II would be towed out of Finland’s territorial waters on Saturday night, in the eighteen-outlet account above and in the wire account filed below; the situation report carries the same plan in the Border Guard’s terms—transfer of the disabled vessel into Russia’s maritime area of responsibility by tow—with Finnish, Estonian and Russian response assets joined in the operation and a restricted area standing around the ship. No account in the file at the midnight stamp reports the tow under way, completed, or postponed. The day therefore closes with the ship’s removal announced and unconfirmed, and the desk files it exactly so.

On responsibility, the day’s last word is firmer than its first and still short of a claim. A Border Guard officer confirmed that a Ukrainian drone strike hit the ship in the morning and caused the fire, in the eighteen-outlet account; Finland’s armed forces spokesman’s judgement stands beside it—it is likely that Ukraine is behind the attack, with no conclusive evidence—in the Baltic account; and Kyiv has claimed nothing in any account in the file. The cargo stands undisclosed: there is no confirmed information about what is on board, and military materiel cannot be ruled out, the Border Guard’s commander said, in the same account; the vessel’s own record stands in the ship’s entry filed below—a roll-on/roll-off vessel shuttling between St. Petersburg and Kaliningrad since October 2022. The suspected helicopter violation stands as the evening filed it: about 4.1 kilometres inside Finnish territory near Loviisa shortly after midday, under Border Guard investigation. Settled: the strike, the drift, the anchorage, the evacuation, the fire at the turn, and the tow’s announcement. Open: the tow itself, the cargo, and responsibility in Kyiv’s word.

Reuters — US House unconstitutionally passed spending bill in 2022, court rules

The House unconstitutionally passed the $1.7 trillion funding bill of 2022 through the pandemic-era proxy rule, the Fifth Circuit ruled on Friday, 9 to 8, upholding an injunction against enforcement of the pregnant workers provision the bill carried. The relief is narrow: the Justice Department and the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission are barred from enforcing the provision against Texas.
