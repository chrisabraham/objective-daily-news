The Kraków Suspect Dies in Hospital, the Mediator Comes to Washington, and Asia Buys Back Friday

Kraków at 0400: The Suspect Is Dead, and the Questions Stay With the Living

Poland’s second day of national mourning began with an announcement from the police who shot the man. “We would like to inform you that the perpetrator of the tragic events died in hospital early this morning,” Małopolska police wrote on X, in the accounts filed below. The man, 24, named in Polish media as Michał O., had been in critical condition since Saturday, when officers shot him within minutes of the first shots in the Main Market Square. He had not been formally arrested and had not been questioned; prosecutors had said both waited on his medical condition. That waiting is now permanent. The investigation the national prosecutor’s office opened as a terrorist offence covers two acts, the killing of six people in the square with the attempted killing of many others including police officers, and the killing of the three at the home; with the suspect dead, its work is the work the overnight accounts list: the full sequence, the source of the weapons, the meaning of the emblems, and whether anyone else took part in planning.

The morning’s new evidence is clothing and guns, and both arrive attributed and unfinished. Interior ministry spokeswoman Karolina Galecka told TOK FM radio on Monday that the man’s clothes carried many different emblems, partly examined and interpreted, the patches associated with various motifs that specialists are still analysing; she would not say more while the investigation runs. The minister’s Saturday reading stands where it stood: fascination with far-right ideology and very radical, ultra-nationalistic views, in the interior minister’s words, and a reading is what it remains. On the weapons, one Monday account counts 11 firearms recovered in all, four from the man or his car in Kraków and seven from the family home, in the victims and firearms account filed below; Polish media report he held a firearms permit and may have obtained guns from the home, and police have not confirmed that account. The wounded stand at 17 injured in the prosecutors’ count carried in the morning accounts, against 15 and 16 in the weekend’s files, with the two officers’ conditions divided in the accounts between regained consciousness in the evening file and one officer in a coma in the Monday account; both are printed in the Cocked Hat. The six of the square stand as the midnight edition named them by age and nationality, and the three at home stand at father 53, mother 52 and sister 21 in the Sunday confirmation the morning accounts repeat. Kraków’s own mourning runs through November 11; the national mourning ends Monday night.

Khor Mor at 0400: The Fire Is Doused, the Assessment Begins, and No Hand Is Claimed

The gas field’s operator has spoken, and its statement is the night’s firmest new document in the region. Dana Gas, joint operator of Khor Mor, said in a Monday statement that production was halted on Sunday following a suspected drone attack, that it was assessing the damage after dousing a fire sparked by the attack, and that there were no injuries to staff. The statement confirms the halt from the party that runs the field; it gives no restart date and no damage figure. The rest of the file stands as Sunday left it: a single drone struck a liquefied petroleum gas storage tank, in the electricity ministry sources’ account; employees were evacuated to shelters; gas supply stands suspended to power plants in Erbil, Sulaimaniyah, Bazian, Chamchamal and Duhok; the loss stands at 2,500 to 2,800 megawatts, with more than 70 percent of the region without power in the regional accounts, in the Al Jazeera account and the Press TV account filed below. Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi’s joint investigative committee is ordered; an initial assessment found no casualties. No group has claimed the attack, and no authority has named a hand. The field was shut once before this year, in July, on security threats, and restarted later that month; that history is in the operator’s account, and it is a history, not a forecast.

What the mediator carries into the room is a sequence, and what he carries out of it is not yet in any file. Mississippi stands at Scott Colom 467,930 to Cindy Hyde-Smith 465,356, a margin of 2,574 on the tally stamped Friday evening; Alaska stands at Mary Peltola ahead of Dan Sullivan by 637 first-choice votes on a count posted Wednesday, in the count guide and the delay account filed below. Reuters, via The Business Standard — how a digital bank’s collapse toppled Paraguay’s president

For years, Paraguay’s digital bank, ueno, attracted roughly half the country’s population to open accounts and gave many people the opportunity to hold a bank account for the first time. But over just five days this week, the bank collapsed, freezing the savings of nearly 3 million customers in a country of 6.5 million people and triggering the country’s deepest political crisis in more than a decade. On Friday evening, President Santiago Peña was impeached after attempting to resign, and his vice-president, Pedro Alliana, was sworn in as his replacement. Authorities quickly arrested Peña; prosecutors charged him on Saturday with breach of trust and administration for personal gain, and a judge ordered him held in pre-trial detention at a military facility. Peña has denied wrongdoing, and his attorney, Ricardo Preda, told journalists that he was pleading not guilty. The central bank said on Saturday that the equivalent of $160 million had since been paid out to nearly 1.2 million savers under the state deposit guarantee scheme.
