Washington at 0400: A Lifeline Named and Sanctioned at $14.7 Billion, and a Root Cause Still Unreleased With a Pause Asked on the Routers

The administration imposed sanctions on more than two dozen exchange houses, front companies and individuals, in the sanctions account filed below, under Operation Economic Outcast, targeting networks across Iran, the United Arab Emirates, Turkey and Hong Kong that use thousands of shell companies to move export earnings and settle payments. Secretary Scott Bessent’s words are in the account: “Iran’s economy is underwater, and its leaders are panicking. Iran’s shadow banking system is the last financial lifeline of a bankrupt regime.” Treasury identified approximately $14.7 billion in transactions potentially linked to the shadow banking networks between January 2025 and August 2026, in the sanctions account and in the follow account filed below. The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network’s form of the same money routes it from banks in China, Singapore and the United Kingdom to institutions in the United Arab Emirates, with $11.3 billion involving companies in the Emirates and about $1.8 billion recorded at Banque Misr involving 103 companies, in the FinCEN account filed below.
