Moscow at Evening: The Envoys Are in Dublin, the Trilateral Is Restarted, and Europe’s Chair Is Two Sentences

Reuters — Spain approves one of the housing decrees that triggered the snap election

Spanish lawmakers on Thursday approved one of two housing decrees whose rejection by parliament last month triggered a November 29 early general election, a day after Wednesday’s general strike over housing costs. By a one-vote margin, the standing body of 69 caretaker deputies passed the decree, which includes rent caps, a temporary ban on evictions and limits on corporate purchases of residential property. The measures came into effect temporarily when the decrees were published in the official gazette in early October.
